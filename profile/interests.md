# Interests and Hobbies

- **Strength training and hypertrophy** — currently running a [[Health/Fitness/Workouts/Full Body|Full Body rotation]] with 3 variants + a dedicated [[Health/Fitness/Workouts/Weakpoint Day|Weakpoint Day]]. 264 tracked sessions over 23 months documented in [[Health/Fitness/_Overview|Fitness Overview]]; full training evolution and progression at [[Health/Fitness/Training Evolution]] and [[Health/Fitness/Progression]]. Genuinely interested in the underlying physiology, recovery science, and programming theory. Long-term aspiration is a **Third Space** membership in London once income allows — views it as one of the "modern establishments" worth investing in for both equipment quality and environment. See [[Profile/Lifestyle Preferences|Lifestyle Preferences]] for how gym access factors into city grading.
- **Boxing and Muay Thai** — training for practical self-defence, conditioning, and cardio rather than competition. Does not require a serious martial arts scene in a city to be happy there. *Not yet practised regularly; flagged as aspiration in [[Health/Cardio and Activity/Insights#activity-gap-analysis|Cardio Insights]].*
- **Swimming**: strong swimmer since age 3, competent in all strokes. Won swimming competitions through primary and secondary school; stopped competing in secondary because competitors got significantly taller and the height differential made winning at higher levels unrealistic (170 cm; see [[Profile/Professional/Personal#Height|Personal]] for the broader geographic-filter angle). Decision was about competitive ceiling, not loss of love for the sport. Still regards swimming as a primary cardio modality and would do it heavily where access permits. **Relevance to current housing decisions:** [[Housing/Stratford/Coppermaker/_Overview|Coppermaker]]'s residents' pool is a meaningful cardio-mode unlock given this background, not a generic amenity, and weights into the comparison against [[Housing/Stratford/East-Village/_Overview|East Village]] and [[Housing/Stratford/Rondo/_Overview|Rondo]] which lack pools.
- **Working from cafés, coworking spaces, hotel lobbies, and libraries** — a core daily habit rather than an occasional thing. Large, air-conditioned, wifi-equipped cafés with plenty of seating are the preferred environment (Café Nero, Starbucks, Black Sheep Coffee). The background noise and ability to people-watch soothe ADHD/autism and aid focus rather than distract. This is the primary reason he bought a MacBook — portability and battery life to support café-based work sessions. Always orders something while there (matcha latte is the typical pick); the drink is a functional focus tool rather than discretionary, see the `feedback_matcha_focus_tool` memory entry. See [[Profile/Lifestyle Preferences|Lifestyle Preferences]] for how café culture factors into city grading.
- **Evening and between-session walks** — used as decompression and a form of "mindful cardio" rather than exercise proper. Particularly enjoys regenerated urban greenery (Nine Elms, Kings Cross, Stratford / E20 and the Olympic Park, York's riverside walks) for these. **Currently constrained by Croydon street-safety** (street feel + face-covered crowds at night); daily step baseline ~2-3k until the 2026 move. Expects significant expansion post-move, especially around Stratford / Olympic Park where the area is felt to be safe and green.
- **Cycling (aspiration)**: wants to learn cycling, intended primarily as outdoor cardio and active transport once relocated to an area felt to be safe. Stratford itself plus the Olympic Park is the planned learning ground given outdoor-friendly geography. *Not yet practised; aspirational, similar in shape to the Boxing/Muay Thai entry above.*
- **Thunderstorms and harsh weather** — actively enjoys thunderstorms and misses them in London compared to Istanbul. Likes cold, snow, and dramatic weather generally. Not a factor in career decisions, but a real aesthetic preference.
- **Game modding** — 18+ released mods across RimWorld, Minecraft, Terraria, and Escape from Tarkov, totalling over 150,000 combined downloads. The original outlet for the same drive that now produces [[Profile/Projects|systems projects]]: understanding and extending complex software at a low level.
- **Cooking** — a genuine creative outlet, not a chore. Loves it, but pragmatic about time — effort scales with the day. On treat days: composed plates with 3-5 components, scratch sauces (chimichurri, fresh pesto, glazes), and deliberate presentation. On regular days: mince over rice, simple but well-seasoned. The core philosophy is **"homemade takeaway"** — recreating restaurant meals (burgers, ramen, steak dinners, pesto pasta) at home so he controls exact macros while eating food that tastes indulgent. This bridges the gap between tracking macros and actually enjoying food. Heavy seasoning is universal regardless of effort level (cracked pepper, chilli flakes, sesame seeds, fresh herbs, spring onions). Strong preference for charring and caramelisation. Dietary rule of thumb: every meal has a protein source, veg/fruit, and complex or simple carbs. Diet consists of protein, healthy fats, fruits, vegetables, and complex carbs — protein-dominant but no carb avoidance despite running a recomp cut. Go-to staples: tenderstem broccoli (veg), sweet potato (carb), chicken thighs and beef mince (protein). Buys high-protein versions of staples when available (protein milk, protein granola, protein bread rolls). Shops across Lidl, Sainsbury's, and M&S in London; see [[Health/Nutrition/Groceries/_Overview|Groceries Tracking]] for the active cost-comparison project. Meal repertoire documented in [[Health/Nutrition/Meals|Meals]].

- **Reading** — currently working through Camus, Kafka, and Dostoevsky. Broader interest in philosophy, scientific writing, and explanations that go a layer deeper than the surface.
- **Cross-disciplinary science** — mathematics, physics, biology, chemistry, particularly when concepts connect across domains.

This is separable from wanting to *build* frontier tech and should be treated as a lifestyle preference that shapes which cities make him feel "not left behind." See [[Profile/Lifestyle Preferences|Lifestyle Preferences]] for how this modulates city grading.
- **AI in healthcare as long-term career direction** — two angles named in [[Profile/Professional/LinkedIn|LinkedIn About]]: personalised medicine (ML over individual physiological data to surface insights, support clinical decisions, and respect privacy) and consumer health tooling (Apple Health / Bevel-shaped adaptive systems over physiological signals — sleep, HRV, training load, recovery — that understand the individual rather than applying population averages). Distinct from "hobbies" or "lifestyle interests"; this is the direction Caner wants to grow into post-portfolio. - **Terminal UI engineering with Ratatui + Crossterm** — demonstrated across multiple projects in active development. [[Projects/Nyquestro/Work/HFT Observability Dashboard|Nyquestro's HFT observability dashboard]] scaffolds a Ratatui-based TUI for latency percentiles and fill-rate; [[Projects/Cernio/_Overview|Cernio's]] debug view uses Crossterm event handling. Adjacent to systems programming and observable-software focus — TUIs are the right shape for tools that need to be running while the developer works on something else, with rich live state visible at a glance.
- **Interactive learning substrate engineering** — [[Projects/Tessarix/_Overview|Tessarix]] is a local-first Tauri 2 + React 19 + MDX desktop substrate teaching abstract technical concepts (image quality, linear algebra, ML) through narrative MDX lessons fused with embedded interactive widgets and LLM-integrated assessment surfaces. M1 shipped across 48 hours via parallel-agent execution: 9 MDX lessons (first being A-FINE, cross-referencing the merged [[Projects/Open Source Contributions/Repos/Burn|burn]] PR #4894), 53 reusable widgets, and three LLM-integrated assessment features. Three-pillar architecture (Teach, Quiz, Interview) over shared content + question bank treats pedagogy as a systems problem, not a prose-only explanation surface.
